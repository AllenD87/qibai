## 中文

### 我们的承诺

为了让每个人都愿意参与，我们承诺：无论年龄、体型、可见或不可见的残障、族裔、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育背景、社会经济状况、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教或性取向，参与本项目都不受骚扰。

### 期望的行为

- 对技术问题就事论事，批评代码与方案，不针对人；
- 尊重不同经验水平，新手提问不嘲讽；
- 接受维护者基于项目范围的取舍；
- 引用他人内容注明来源，不把他人的成果说成自己的。

### 不可接受的行为

- 人身攻击、侮辱、贬低、骚扰、跟踪、未经同意公开他人隐私信息；
- 性暗示或色情内容、以及任何未获同意的性关注；
- 政治攻击、歧视性言论、仇恨言论；
- 恶意刷屏、灌水 issue、故意提交破坏性 PR；
- 在本项目讨论区发布商业推广、诱导下载、钓鱼链接；
- 其他在专业场合明显不当的行为。

### 适用范围

适用于仓库的 issue、pull request、discussion、commit 评论，以及以本项目名义参与的任何公开场合。

### 处理方式

发现问题请私信维护者（<alunupc@126.com>），不要公开对线。维护者会据情节采取：提醒、要求修改、删除内容、临时限制、永久禁止。
维护者本人也受本准则约束，处理与自身相关的投诉时会回避。

### 归属

本准则参考 Contributor Covenant v2.1 的核心条款，按本项目（个人开源项目、中文为主）的实际场景做了精简。

## English

### Our pledge

To make participation welcoming for everyone, we pledge that involvement in this project is free of harassment regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

### Expected behaviour

- Argue about the technical problem, not the person — critique code and designs;
- Respect different experience levels; never mock beginners for asking;
- Accept maintainers' scope decisions for the project;
- Attribute other people's work; do not present it as your own.

### Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, insults, belittling, harassment, stalking, or publishing others' private information without consent;
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention of any kind;
- Political attacks, discriminatory or hateful speech;
- Deliberate spam, flooding issues, or intentionally destructive pull requests;
- Commercial promotion, drive-by downloads or phishing links in project discussion spaces;
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

### Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit comments in this repository, and to any public space where someone represents this project.

### Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer (<alunupc@126.com>) rather than arguing in public. Depending on severity the maintainer may warn, request changes, remove content, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban.
The maintainer is bound by this code too, and will recuse themselves from handling complaints about their own conduct.

### Attribution

This code is a condensed version of the core terms of Contributor Covenant v2.1, adapted to the realities of a personal open-source project that is primarily Chinese-language.
